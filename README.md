# Platinum Pawn — Forge-CT functional demo

A working customer experience for **Platinum Pawn**, two Connecticut pawn shops:

- Bristol — 294 Middle Street — 860-261-4031
- New Britain — 57 Broad Street — 860-223-2222

This is a pitch demo. It is not the live Platinum Pawn website.

## What to try

1. Open the site
2. Understand **Pawn / Sell / Shop / Visit**
3. Choose **Sell**
4. Choose **Gold / Jewelry**
5. Choose a location
6. See photo guidance
7. Understand that the **final offer happens in person**
8. Submit
9. See confirmation with **Call this location** and **Get directions**

Quote submissions stay in the browser. They are not sent to Platinum Pawn.

## What this is not

- Not an online appraisal or gold calculator
- Not fake inventory or ecommerce
- Not fake reviews, awards, or pricing

## Source of truth

Verified from the public site and the Forge-CT master audit. Hours, addresses, pawn terms, and item categories are published information. Where something is uncertain, the demo does not invent it.

## Stack

TanStack Start, React 19, Tailwind v4. Built for Forge-CT.
